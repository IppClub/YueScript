## About

<img width="1071" height="1500" decoding="async" src="/image/mascot/electrichearts_20260211A_yuescript_xiaoyu.png" alt="Xiaoyu the Cyber Rabbit" style="max-width: 420px; width: 100%; border-radius: 12px; margin-bottom: 1.5em;" />

Xiaoyu (小玉, "little jade") is a cyber rabbit and the official mascot of the YueScript project. She sits on a crescent moon, writing YueScript on her laptop.

Her full title is Xiaoyu the Cyber Rabbit (机兔小玉). She reflects what YueScript aims for: clean, expressive, and playful.

**[Tyson Tan](https://tysontan.com)** designed and illustrated Xiaoyu for the project free of charge. We are grateful for his contribution.
